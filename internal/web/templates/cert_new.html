1. How do you want to supply the key?
2. Your certificate signing request

Generate one with: openssl req -new -newkey rsa:2048 -nodes -keyout my.key -out my.csr

Only supply this if you want goca to store the key so you can re-download the pair later. It is verified against the CSR and encrypted at rest.

2. Subject

Required when generating. When signing a CSR, leave the subject fields blank to keep what the CSR carries.

3. Certificate options

Comma separated. Types are detected automatically, or prefix explicitly with DNS:, IP:, EMAIL:, URI:. When signing a CSR, filling this in replaces the CSR's own SANs.
