The openssl equivalent

Certificate
openssl x509 -in cert.pem -text -noout
CSR
openssl req -in file.csr -text -noout
Private key
openssl pkey -in file.key -text -noout
Does a key match a cert?
openssl x509 -noout -pubkey -in cert.pem | openssl pkey -pubin -outform der | sha256sum

Paste the certificate and key together above and the "Matches" line does that last comparison for you.
